# Notification policy

Telegram is the primary alert channel. The owner-only Mac configuration holds the bot credentials. GPT-6 Sol researches each shortlisted book before a book alert is queued. The 12 source lanes keep scanning; the current policy changes the editorial decision, not the discovery schedule.

From 29 September 2026, `collector-editorial-v3` follows the earlier GitHub-to-ChatGPT review style. The model checks the actual offered copy, collector relevance, edition, condition, price and available market evidence. Core 175 tiers, Parr/Badger, Roth 101 and the local library help identify important books but are not a whitelist. Tier 2, Tier 3 and overlooked photographers can qualify. A famous name, generic anthology or routine reprint alone does not.

- 🦄 **Unicorn** and 💎 **gem** require a clearly identified fixed-price book and a checked like-for-like price at least 25% higher than the item's asking price. A confirmed gem has a checked sold price; asking-only evidence is labelled **possible gem**. Unicorns need a rarer, larger anomaly and stronger support.
- 📚⭐ **Collector pick** is an important, appealing copy at a sensible price. Signed, first and limited editions can qualify without a proved resale profit, including inexpensive copies with no checked sale. Asking-only or absent sale evidence is labelled as uncertain. A checked comparable can show a modest gap; the book's importance and edition still matter.
- 🔎 **Investigate** is a specific plausible opportunity with an edition, signature, condition, market or availability question. It says what to check. Auctions can appear here; their current bid is explicitly provisional and no automatic bid ceiling is claimed.

The cash ceiling remains £200 estimated all-in. Known incoming postage is included; unknown postage uses a £20 budget allowance. Price comparisons use the *item price* before incoming postage, while the alert shows the estimated total. GBP priced listings currently qualify for phone alerts. The old 40% discount, £50 resale margin and Tier 1 only thresholds are retained in configuration for rollback compatibility but are not alert gates under this policy.

Each phone message shows the item price and postage, the lowest checked like-for-like price when available, photographer tier and bibliography flag when known, one reason and one check. Asking prices are identified as asking prices, and absent market evidence is stated plainly. The seller and checked comparison links are buttons. The delivery worker rechecks the current observation, owner decisions, live eBay price and postage or non-eBay source freshness. Imported historical stock stays silent. A reply with a photo still triggers an image assessment using the latest collector review. No purchase, bid or offer is automated.

Only `BARGAIN_FIND` events from the current policy can start a book alert. Older queued policy events are suppressed on delivery. The local scanner has no daily AI research job cap; Codex account limits can temporarily defer queued research. Telegram provider acceptance does not prove receipt on the phone.
