# Notification policy

Telegram is the primary channel. Owner-only local files hold the bot token and chat ID. The default repository configuration disables delivery; the Mac's private production configuration enables it.

From 28 September 2026, a book reaches Telegram only after GPT-6 Sol identifies the actual offered copy and the local bargain screen confirms one of two collector opportunities. **Flip gems and unicorns** require a strong estimated resale margin. **Collection priorities** are specifically curated books by Tier 1 photographers that may be worth buying to keep even without resale profit. A famous Tier 1 name on a routine book does not qualify. The 175-name tier list, Parr/Badger and Roth 101 guide discovery and context but do not automatically qualify a listing. All 12 source lanes continue scanning broadly. Interesting or uncertain books remain in the local dashboard without a phone alert. Publisher and prize discoveries also remain there.

Both routes require a GBP fixed-price listing, current source evidence, and an all-in purchase of at most £200. The screen checks direct public market pages for the same edition in no better condition, including a sold GBP price and date within three years. The flip route requires at least two checked prices, a purchase at least 40% below the lowest, and at least £50 indicative net resale room after a 20% selling-fee allowance, £10 outgoing postage and known incoming postage (or a £20 buffer). The collection route needs a curated Tier 1 work, a seller-supported edition, at least one checked sold comparable and a price at least 20% below it; it has no profit test. These values can be changed under `[policy]` in the private `config.toml`. Public listing pages can be incomplete; this is a screening estimate, not a guaranteed resale value or profit. Auctions stay in the dashboard because the current bid is not a final buy price.

Only `BARGAIN_FIND` events from the current `collector-bargains-v6` review may initiate a book alert. The message distinguishes a 💎/🦄 flip from a 📚⭐ collection priority and gives the all-in estimate, discount to the checked comparable, brief photographer/book context, one opportunity reason and one risk. Flip alerts include indicative resale room; collection alerts explicitly omit it. Buttons link to the seller and sold comparable. The delivery worker rechecks the review, current observation, owner decisions, eBay live price and postage, and source freshness. Old queued policy events are suppressed. Imported historical stock stays silent. Ambiguous Telegram delivery gets at most one clearly marked possible-duplicate retry; provider acceptance is not proof of phone receipt. No purchase, bid or offer is automated.

Replying with a photo or screenshot to an accepted old `RESEARCHED_FIND` or new `BARGAIN_FIND` alert still triggers an image-based Telegram reply. The local scanner has no daily AI research job cap. Lead research, photo replies and wider sweeps run through one research worker; photo replies have priority. Actual Codex account limits still apply. On a provider limit, work stays queued and the worker retries after a temporary backoff. No unresearched book alert is sent. A standalone photo cannot reliably identify its target listing.
