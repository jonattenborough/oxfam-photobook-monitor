# Notification policy

Jon selected Telegram as the primary channel. The bot token and private chat ID are stored locally. Telegram accepted one labelled setup message (message ID 3); Jon confirmed receipt on his phone. The default `config.toml` keeps delivery disabled, and shadow mode cannot send automatic events. Pushover is implemented as an optional adapter and remains unused.

From 28 September 2026, Telegram uses a research-first collector policy. Local matching shortlists fresh listings, including plausible unfamiliar books and uncertain editions. GPT-6 Sol assesses whether the actual item and offered copy fit Jon's documentary/street/humanist/socially engaged/portrait/colour collecting interests. The 175-name tier list and photobook canon are useful context, not an exclusion list. A credible underdescribed find may receive a cautious PAY_ATTENTION alert even without a proven first edition or official book page. Routine reprints, generic anthologies, unrelated mentions and ordinary books should receive PASS. The daily research limit is 48 jobs, with most capacity reserved for individual listings; exhaustion delays research and never sends an unresearched alert.

Only `RESEARCHED_FIND` events with a `PAY_ATTENTION`, `GEM`, or `UNICORN` verdict may reach the phone, with at most one active/accepted finding per listing. Unproven GEM and UNICORN claims are downgraded to PAY_ATTENTION. The short alert gives the observed item price, postage if known, tier and Parr/Badger context where applicable, one reason to look, the edition caveat, the main risk, and the listing link. It does not claim a percentage discount without like-for-like market evidence. The delivery worker rechecks the review, current observation, freshness and owner decisions. eBay listings also require an exact live Browse check; other seller feeds require a recent capture and an availability caveat. Old queued `FAST_LEAD`, `RESEARCH_UPDATE`, publisher and sweep events are suppressed. Imported historical stock is silent. An ambiguous provider result gets at most one later retry, with an explicit possible-duplicate label; a second unknown result stays `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN` for inspection. Provider acceptance is distinct from a phone receipt. No purchase, bid or offer is automated.

All 12 source lanes continue collecting candidates. Publisher and prize discoveries remain in the dashboard. Recent non-eBay seller listings can also reach the research queue; the alert asks Jon to confirm availability on the seller page. Research sweeps run less frequently to reserve most of the 48 daily research slots for actual listings.

The first phone test is a clearly labelled setup message, not a purchase recommendation. Jon confirmed this setup test arrived on his phone. A direct seller-link test is still pending. Quiet hours, emergency acknowledgement and repeat policy remain unconfigured, so no emergency repeats are sent.
